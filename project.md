# Final Project

The final project is a substantial piece of work that demonstrates your ability to apply knowledge and skills to a real problem.

## 1. Proposal

Your proposal should include:

- Problem statement
- Motivation
- Related work
- Proposed approach
- Expected outcome
- Timeline

## 2. Development

You should maintain a regular development process and meet with your supervisor regularly.

## 3. Evaluation

Your project should include a systematic evaluation.

## 4. Final Report

The final report should document:

- Background
- Related work
- Methodology
- Implementation
- Evaluation
- Discussion
- Conclusion
