# 9. Resources

This page is a hub for the kinds of materials students need as they move from an initial interest to a focused research or project direction. 

## Available resources

- [Proposal template](proposal-template.md)
- [Research paper reading guide](paper-reading-guide.md)
- [Meeting template](meeting-template.md)

## What this hub is for

The resource hub is designed to support students with:

- preparing a proposal
- learning how to read and evaluate research papers
- planning and documenting meetings
- building a practical routine for early-stage graduate work

## Planned additions

This hub will continue to grow with additional pages such as:

- reading lists
- literature review templates
- progress report templates
- learning packages
