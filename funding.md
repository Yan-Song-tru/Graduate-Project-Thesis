# 4. Funding and Graduate Student Opportunities

Funding arrangements vary from year to year. They may depend on research grants, internal research funding, scholarships, teaching opportunities, and your circumstances. If you are considering joining my group, please contact me so we can discuss what opportunities are currently available rather than relying on a general statement on this page.

## Current research funding

I currently hold an NSERC Discovery Grant titled *Temporal Detection of Human Actions in Videos with Multimodal Data and Limited Labels*. This grant supports research related to computer vision, video understanding, multimodal data, and learning when labelled data are limited.

The grant may create opportunities for research assistant work or related graduate projects, depending on available funding, project fit, student qualifications, and program requirements. Holding this grant does not by itself guarantee admission, a funded position, or a particular thesis or project topic.

Depending on the situation, support may involve one or more of the following:

- Research assistant positions supported by research grants
- University, government, or external scholarships
- Teaching assistant opportunities
- A combination of funding sources over the course of the degree

Funding may be competitive and is not necessarily guaranteed. When funding is tied to research assistant work, I will explain the responsibilities connected to the project. These may include implementing methods, preparing data, running experiments, documenting results, or contributing to research outputs.

The expected duration and conditions of support should be discussed early and revisited as circumstances change. International and domestic students may face different eligibility rules, deadlines, and financial planning considerations, so please review the relevant university and scholarship requirements as well.

For questions about current opportunities, please email [ysong@tru.ca](mailto:ysong@tru.ca).
