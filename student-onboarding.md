# Student Onboarding Checklist

This checklist is intended to help new students get oriented quickly and start contributing in a clear, structured way.

## Before the first meeting
- Review the project goals and research direction.
- Read the key papers or background material identified by the supervisor.
- Clarify whether the project is a thesis, applied project, or research assistant role.
- Learn the expected meeting schedule and communication expectations.

## First week
- Confirm the main project topic or working problem.
- Understand the research question or goal.
- Set up any required tools, software, or computing environment.
- Review the timeline for early milestones.

## First month
- Read a small set of relevant papers.
- Summarize the key ideas in your own words.
- Identify at least one gap, question, or challenge in the current literature.
- Start a simple draft of the project plan or proposal.

## Ongoing expectations
- Keep notes from readings and meetings.
- Track your progress and next steps.
- Raise questions early when something is unclear.
- Balance reading, implementation, and reflection.

## Useful habits for early-stage researchers

- Maintain a research notebook or notes file.
- Document what you tried and what you learned.
- Keep a list of the main papers you read.
- Be clear about what is known, what is uncertain, and what needs to be tested.

## Recommended early questions

- What is the central research problem?
- What has already been done?
- What am I expected to produce in the next few weeks?
- What is my current plan, and what is the next concrete step?
- What would success look like at this stage?
