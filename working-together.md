# 8. How We Work Together

## Communication

I usually communicate with students by email or Microsoft Teams. Please check at least one of these channels regularly and reply in a timely and professional way. Even a brief acknowledgment is helpful if you need more time to provide a full response. I do not expect you to be available constantly, but I do need to know that important messages have reached you and that issues affecting your progress are being communicated early.

During active research, we will typically meet regularly, often weekly. The exact schedule may change as the project moves through different phases. Before each meeting, I ask you to be ready to discuss:

- What you have accomplished
- What you learned
- Problems you encountered
- What you plan to do next

## A simple meeting structure

- Since the last meeting
- Results and findings
- Problems and questions
- Decisions needed
- Next steps

## What I will do as your supervisor

I will:

- Provide research direction and feedback
- Help refine research questions
- Recommend relevant literature and methods
- Review important research documents
- Help troubleshoot research problems
- Support professional development
- Provide timely feedback whenever reasonably possible

## What I ask from you

I ask you to:

- Take ownership of your project or research
- Maintain regular progress
- Read independently
- Document experiments carefully
- Keep code, data, and results organized
- Meet agreed deadlines
- Communicate problems early
- Prepare for meetings
- Maintain academic and research integrity

## Please communicate early

Do not wait until the next meeting to tell me about a problem that is preventing you from making progress. Please send a brief update before the meeting whenever possible, including your progress, problems encountered, decisions you need help with, and any support you need. This gives me time to review the information and prepare so that our meeting can be productive.

Supervision is a shared commitment. If progress remains substantially delayed despite reasonable support and agreed plans, if communication breaks down, or if there are serious concerns about academic integrity or professional conduct, I reserve the right to discontinue supervision, subject to applicable program regulations and university procedures. I will normally raise concerns early, discuss them with you, and agree on a reasonable plan for improvement before taking such action.
